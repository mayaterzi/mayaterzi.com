Maya Terzi
I'm a creative director and founder working across fashion, branding, and editorial storytelling. My practice sits between design and narrative, building visual worlds that feel both personal and cultural.

I founded Terzi Evi, a brand rooted in heritage craftsmanship and contemporary Mediterranean influence, alongside Cha$e a bag co., a streetwear-driven nonprofit focused on community and visual identity. Additionally, I serve as Co-founder and Creative Lead for The Walk Thru, an editorial platform exploring the intersection of fashion, sport, and culture.

Alongside my entrepreneurial work, I have over a year of experience at Manejah PR, where I develop social media strategy, creative campaigns, and branded content for clients across interior design, lifestyle, hospitality, and real estate. From content planning and copywriting to campaign execution and analytics, I help shape digital brand identities through thoughtful storytelling and strategic creative direction.

On track to obtain my B.S. in Creative Industries and Marketing at Bentley University this December 2026, I'm interested in how design builds meaning through imagery, construction, and digital culture — and how those worlds translate into physical experience.
